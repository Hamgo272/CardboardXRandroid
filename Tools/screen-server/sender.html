Share your computer screen with CardboardXR

1. Press Share screen and choose Entire screen (not a window) so clicks from the headset map correctly.
2. Open Computer Screen in CardboardXR on your phone. The phone can find this computer automatically when both are on the same Wi-Fi network.
3. The first time, approve the phone below. Only approved devices can view the screen or control the mouse.
4. Keep this page open while sharing. Computer audio continues to play from the computer.
